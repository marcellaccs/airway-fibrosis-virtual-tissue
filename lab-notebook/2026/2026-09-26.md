## Results: the new PDFs against open validation items

**Zou et al. (2014)** — closes the "direction only, no percentage" gap in §3.1, and it is the
wood-smoke-specific anchor:

- Small airway wall thickness: control **7.6 ± 1.1** vs WS **14.3 ± 3.3** vs CS **12.1 ± 3.4**
  µm²/µm of basement-membrane perimeter (mean ± SEM, n = 8/group, p < 0.01). Ratio WS:control
  ≈ **1.9×**.
- Total collagen (Masson) in the small airway wall: control **2.4 ± 1.1** vs WS **4.9 ± 2.2** vs
  CS **4.9 ± 1.0** µm²/µm (p < 0.05). Ratio ≈ **2.0×**.
- Type I collagen (IHC): control **7.0 ± 2.1** vs WS **11.1 ± 2.7** vs CS **10.5 ± 2.5** µm²/µm
  (p < 0.05). Ratio ≈ **1.6×**.
- **No significant difference at 4 months; significant at 7 months.** This is the late > early
  ordering target (minimal set item 4), from the matched exposure rather than from Li et al. (2024).
- Mean linear intercept: 47.7 ± 4.0 (control) vs 60.5 ± 6.7 (WS) vs 56.1 ± 4.1 (CS) µm.
- ASM: **not significantly thickened** by WS or CS. Supports A8's frozen boundary standing in for
  ASM in v1 and argues the v2 ASM compartment is not required for this exposure's lesion.
- Units caveat: collagen is reported **per mm of basement-membrane perimeter (µm²/µm)**, not as an
  area fraction. It is a thickness-like quantity, so it is comparable to the model's subepithelial
  thickness readout and to a ratio, not to the Kong/Islam percentage band. The ~3× Kong-implied
  ratio in §3.1 should be **revised down to ~1.6–2.0× for the exposure-matched anchor**.
- MMP9/MMP2/TIMP1 all up, localized to airway epithelium. Confirms the no-MMP v1 omission is a
  real limitation in exactly this model, as §4 already states.
- EMT: E-cadherin/vimentin double-positive subepithelial cells in only **3 of 8** WS rats. Weak,
  and supports A17's deferral of EMT.

**Eapen et al. (2021)** — the §6 follow-up item ("pull full text for thickness and αSMA⁺/mm²") is
only **partly** closable:

- Lamina propria thickness: "at least a **10-fold** change overall" vs controls; adventitia 2–3×;
  smooth muscle +50%. The LP correlation axis runs **5–25 µm** (Fig 5e), so pathological LP is
  roughly 10–25 µm.
- αSMA⁺ myofibroblasts: axes show **up to ~50 cells/mm of Rbm** and **up to ~2500–3000 cells/mm²
  of LP**, increased in NLFS and both COPD groups. **Group means and SDs are figure-only** — no
  table. So the αSMA⁺/mm² target stays *(verify)* / not plottable, same problem as Tisler 3B.
  Digitizing Figs 1c–e and 3b–d is the only route.
- The correlation structure **is** extractable and is the usable target:
  αSMA⁺ in LP vs LP thickness **r′ = 0.43, p < 0.05**; αSMA⁺ in LP vs FEV1/FVC r′ = −0.80 (COPD-CS)
  and −0.61 (COPD-ES); collagen-1 % in LP vs LP thickness r′ = 0.59 (COPD-CS), p < 0.05, and **not**
  significant in normal-lung-function smokers.
- Collagen-1 % rises only **~1.5×** in the lamina propria but **5–6×** in the adventitia;
  fibronectin rises more uniformly. Two consequences: (i) the compartment where the collagen signal
  is largest is the **adventitia**, which v1 does not represent (it is the frozen boundary), and
  (ii) fibronectin, not collagen, is the more responsive ECM species, which a single lumped C field
  cannot separate.
- Myofibroblasts are "most concentrated in a **shallow band just deep to the Rbm**" and the increase
  is **not uniform**. This is a directly usable Tier-1/2 spatial target for the model's myofibroblast
  distribution, and it is independent of any unit calibration.

**Tisler et al. (2020)** — full text now in the repo. Confirms what the 2026-09-07 check found:

## Assessment: research question, novelty, utility

Recorded in full in `docs/novelty_utility_review_2026-09-26.md` (new). Summary:

- The **stated** driving question (antioxidant effect on remodeling, `docs/2025_project_status.md`
  §3) is the weakest of the three candidate questions: it is a spatial re-run of a published ODE
  result, and it is blocked by the unresolved sign problem (spec §6).
- The **defensible** question is the one the validation doc already treats as the headline: does the
  *spatial arrangement* of epithelial damage change the lesion at matched total damage
  (focal vs diffuse), and where is the `S_M2` threshold between the two regimes. That arm is not
  built.
- Prior art is close but not overlapping: Brown (2011) is particulate-driven inflammation/fibrosis
  ABM but non-spatial in the airway-wall sense; Islam (2023) is the closest spatial-source-structure
  result but alveolar/COVID; Cogno (2022), Hall (2024, stretch/stiffness spring-network ABM),
  Warsinske (2016, fibroblast+epithelial co-culture ABM) are parenchymal/IPF. **No agent-based or
  cellular-Potts model of the small-airway wall under biomass-smoke exposure was found.** That
  exposure-plus-compartment combination is the novelty claim, and it is defensible.
- Fit to the Frontiers topic is **partial and needs reframing**: the topic is epithelial–immune
  crosstalk, and in v1 the immune compartment lives entirely in the ODE (A10) and reaches the
  tissue as two scalars. The epithelium is currently a passive GF source with no immune sensing.
