## Decisions
- **Layer order flips so the Player reads top-to-bottom as airway → epithelium →
  subepithelial → frozen wall**, matching the anatomical picture, instead of the old order
  which put the frozen wall at the top of the view.
- **Lumen halved, and the freed rows go to the subepithelial band, not left unclaimed.** Halving
  alone without reallocating would have just been arithmetic; the point was giving the
  subepithelial compartment more room to accumulate mass before running out of space, which is
  exactly what Priority 1's displacement mechanism needs.
- **Apical/lateral/basal compartmentalization: reviewed, not adopted for v1, deferred as a
  realism upgrade.** It would let the model express a real basal→ECM adhesion preference
  (`ClusterId`-grouped apical/lateral/basal sub-cells with `ContactInternal`, per Module 5.1),
  but v1's displacement mechanism is a bulk volume-exclusion effect that doesn't depend on
  sub-cellular polarity, and adopting it now would mean rewriting every place that currently
  loops over the two epithelial types (damage seeding, ECM secretion, sheet-integrity checks)
  to be compartment-aware. Not worth it until the plain-sheet mechanism itself is shown to work.
- **FocalPointPlasticity ("Links") and tissue folding: reviewed, not needed for v1.** Confirmed
  via the Day 6 slides that "Links" is the same FPP plugin Module 7.3 uses for tissue folding —
  both are active-force mechanics (apical constriction, crawling, anchors, convergent-extension).
  v1's mechanism is passive compression/volume-exclusion from an expanding neighbor, already
  expressible with `Contact` + `VolumeLocalFlex`/`SurfaceLocalFlex` + `ConnectivityGlobal` +
  `Freeze`. Tissue folding specifically is also the wrong phenomenon — it's localized bending,
  not the bulk translation of a flat sheet that lumen narrowing needs.
- **Displacement is necessary (it's the model's headline readout, tied to the Higham ~22%
  narrowing validation target) but is not expected to occur yet with current parameters**, for
  two structural reasons found while working through the new geometry's numbers: (1) at
  `FIBROBLAST_INITIAL_NUMBER = 80` and `R_FIB = 0.001`, the subepithelial compartment sits at
  roughly 17–20% occupancy through a full run — nowhere near enough to generate crowding
  pressure against the epithelium; (2) field `C` currently has no volume-occupying
  representation at all — it scales `lambdaVolume` (stiffness) on cells already present, never
  a cell's `targetVolume`, so ECM burden accumulating in `C` has zero mechanical consequence for
  the compartment's footprint. Recorded the fix order in `docs/cc3d_v1_todo.md` PRIORITY 0:
  measure baseline displacement first, build the lumen-area flood-fill readout, then couple
  local `C` to myofibroblast `targetVolume` growth (not just `lambdaVolume`) as the load-bearing
  fix, with raising `R_FIB` and (last resort) a literal volume-occupying ECM cell type held in
  reserve if that's not enough.
- **Noticed but not fixed:** `FIBROBLAST_INITIAL_NUMBER` is 80 in the current file, but the
  2026-08-09 "Completed" section of `cc3d_v1_todo.md` states it was reduced 80 → 10. Flagged in
  the todo doc as an open discrepancy; didn't touch the parameter since it's orthogonal to the
  reorder and I don't know which value was intended.
