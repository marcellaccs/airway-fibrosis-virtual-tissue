# 2026-10-11

**Focus:** Re-evaluate the model's utility in light of a newly published wood-smoke airway-on-a-chip
platform, and decide whether the Frontiers paper's goals should shift.

## The new paper

Bennet, T. J., Randhawa, A., Pieters, A., Caffrey, T. M., Solomon, T., Lyall, E., Huff, R. D.,
Schwartz, C., Xi, Y., Carlsten, C., & Cheung, K. C. (2026). *Engineering a multilayer microfluidic
airway-on-a-chip with tunable GelMA hydrogel for physiologically relevant aerosol exposure studies.*
**Scientific Reports**. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-69468-5 (accepted 27 Aug 2026, published
online 9 Sep 2026). UBC — Cheung (biomedical engineering) and Carlsten (Air Pollution Exposure Lab).

Platform, as built:

- Two vertically stacked PDMS microchannels (1 mm x 1 mm) separated by a 10 µm porous PET membrane
  (0.4 µm pores), culture surface ~18 mm². Upper channel hydraulic diameter 1 mm, chosen to match a
  human bronchiole.
- **Epithelium:** Calu-3 on the ECM-coated membrane, taken to air-liquid interface by the airflow
  system. Confluent monolayer, ZO-1 positive, MUC5AC secretion rising over the culture period.
- **Interstitium:** HFL1 human fetal lung fibroblasts encapsulated in 3D in **6% GelMA / 0.6% LAP**,
  selected from nine formulations for stiffness within the native lung range (**0.44–7.5 kPa**),
  structural stability, and >70% viability. GelMA retains RGD and MMP-sensitive sites.
- **Vasculature:** needle-based sacrificial molding gives a perfusable circular lumen of
  **~250 µm diameter** inside the hydrogel, seeded with HUVECs; vWF detectable only in tri-culture.
- **Flow:** peristaltic perfusion at 5 µL/min; airflow at 10 mL/min per chip giving an estimated wall
  shear of **~0.2 dyn/cm²** across the apical epithelial surface.
- **Exposure:** whole wood smoke from a smoke generator, aerosol delivered at ALI with real-time
  PM2.5 monitoring and controlled dilution. Not an extract.

Results they report:

- TGF-β1 (50 ng/mL, perfused) in fibroblast monoculture: elongation down, F-actin up, **αSMA up**
  (significant), total cells up, no LDH change. In epithelial–fibroblast co-culture: **IL-6 and IL-8
  significantly up**.
- Whole wood smoke in co-culture (15 days of culture before exposure): **IL-6 and IL-8 significantly
  up** (n = 7 pooled over 2 runs); MUC5AC and endogenous TGF-β1 **modestly up but not significant**
  (n = 4 and n = 3, separate experiments); **LDH not significantly raised** — no widespread cell death.
- MMP-2 in effluent stayed **below the assay's lower limit of quantification (0.5 ng/mL)** for the
  whole culture period. They describe this as a functional baseline, and state that minimal bulk ECM
  remodeling occurred during establishment.

## Assessment

Written up in full as `docs/aoc_platform_impact_2026-10-11.md`. The short version: this is a
companion platform, not competing prior art, and the model is **more** useful than it was a week ago,
but two things have to change.

**Why it helps**

- Same compartment, same exposure, same scale. Small conducting airway, epithelium over a
  fibroblast-populated 3D ECM, **whole** wood smoke rather than extract. Nothing else in the
  literature matches the model this closely.
- **Timescales already agree.** Their culture-and-exposure window is 15 days. A 20,000-MCS run at the
  A7 calibration (62.4 s/MCS) is **14.4 days**. This was not arranged and it means model output can be
  reported on their clock with no recalibration.
- **Their TGF-β1 delivery is `S_M2`.** 50 ng/mL perfused uniformly through the upper channel is,
  experimentally, a spatially uniform additive pro-fibrotic source. The uniform pedestal (A14) has
  been written up in the docs as a simplification to apologize for. It is what the best available in
  vitro platform actually does, and in a model-vs-chip comparison it is the matched condition.
- **Their lumen is 250 µm; the model domain is 250 µm wide** (200 px at 1.25 µm/px). Coincidence, but
  a convenient one for any figure that puts the two side by side.
- **GelMA stiffness is tunable and they name the target.** They state that future devices could be
  tuned to the fibrotic ECM range (**~17 kPa**) against the native 0.44–7.5 kPa. That is the v2
  stiffness-gated latent-TGF-β mechanism with an experimental knob attached, which turns it from a
  modeling choice into a testable prediction.

**Why it also hurts, and this is the real finding**

Wood smoke produced inflammatory and pro-fibrotic signaling with **no significant LDH release**.
Signal without measurable cell death.

The v1 model drives everything from `f_d`: damaged epithelium is the dominant stationary GF source
(committed rates: `EpitheliumDamaged` 0.1 vs `EpithelialHealthy` 0.01, a 10:1 ratio), damage is a
one-directional Bernoulli flip, and there is no repair path. If the exposure-driven signal rises
without damage, the damage-gated architecture is at best incomplete. A19's decision that healthy
epithelium secretes at a nonzero rate is **supported**; the 10:1 damaged:healthy ratio is
**challenged**. Logged as a new challenge entry against A14/A19.

**What it does not give**

No collagen quantification, no ECM area fraction, MMP-2 below LLOQ. `Zou2014` remains the only
exposure-matched quantitative anchor and nothing in `validation_targets.md` §8.1 changes.

Their smoke arrives by airflow with gravity-driven deposition across the whole epithelial surface, so
it is a **diffuse-injury** platform. The focal arm has no counterpart on this chip. That protects the
novelty of the focal-vs-diffuse question and simultaneously means the chip cannot validate it.

## Decisions (proposed, for Marcella)

- **Keep focal-vs-diffuse as the headline**, since nothing but a model can test it, but add a second
  section of model predictions for this platform, in quantities they already collect. That section is
  what makes the submission Original Research rather than Hypothesis and Theory, and it fits the
  special issue's epithelial–immune framing better than the mechanics does: IL-6 and IL-8 are
  epithelial-derived cytokine output.
- **Run H1 before any writing starts** (see the impact doc). `f_d = 0` with the `S_M2` pedestal alone,
  pinned seed. No new code. If the lesion still forms, the chip's no-LDH result is reproduced. If it
  does not, the damage-gated architecture needs rethinking, and October is a much better time to learn
  that than March.
- **Stop treating the uniform pedestal as a limitation** in the docs. Reframe it as the matched
  condition for a chip comparison.
- The §3.7 real-sections dependency may be replaceable by a collaboration rather than tissue-bank
  access. Carlsten's group is also a plausible referee for this special issue either way.
