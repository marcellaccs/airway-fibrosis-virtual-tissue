## Decisions
- Each modeling assumption will be presented on the slides as a deliberate choice with a
  supporting citation and, where one exists, a "cf." challenging citation — not asserted as
  settled fact.
- Keep PDGF / IL-4 / IL-13 alongside TGF-β in the tissue model rather than reducing to
  TGF-β alone (Nguyen et al., 2024 shows single-pathway targeting is insufficient in vivo).
- The A/P (M2:M1) ratio stays an explicit modeling posit: literature supports M2 dominance
  being pro-fibrotic directionally, but no source establishes a numeric recruitment threshold.
- New `references.bib` entries use abbreviated authors + blank volume/pages as placeholders;
  final metadata to come from Zotero export (per repo convention), not hand-entry.
