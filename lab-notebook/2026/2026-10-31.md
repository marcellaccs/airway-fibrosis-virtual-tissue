## Decisions

- **Pedestal carries the `WS` gate and the `n_epi/N_band` spread**, and is added to the subepithelial
  band only. Both factors are the ODE's own term written per pixel, not new freedom; the plan's §3
  formula had them implicit (without the spread the pedestal would be ~800× the ODE's ratio to the
  epithelial source).
- **`P` constants are the 10-30 pins, not the §5 placeholders** (D = 0.10 would have been the
  placeholder). Marcella has not yet confirmed the pins; they are one-line changes in `parameters.py`
  plus the matching XML literals, and `start()` warns if the two drift.
- **Per-cell, not per-pixel, epithelial rate**, split over the eligible pixels: the plan says "per cell
  per MCS", and a per-cell rate keeps `P_H` independent of how much of a cell's basal side is free.
- **Lumen `P` is reported, not constrained.** The design goal "`P` in the wall, not the lumen" holds for
  the *writes* (verified: every source pixel has y ≤ 71), but a diffusing field with λ = 80 px fills the
  lumen, and a `DiffusionCoefficient` of 0 in the epithelial types would make the sheet a sink, not a
  wall, and would starve consumer 1 (it reads `P` at the damaged cell's COM). Decision item 5 in the plan.
- The H5 `P`-analogue is defined (first pass) as the run mean of the exposure-gated per-cell epithelial
  production index; Gate C may replace it with the chip-clock time series.

## Results / observations

- **Regression (Phase 1 done-when, part 3): PASS.** Switches off vs HEAD at 3k, seed 200: every one of
  the 27 B4 CSV columns identical (lumen 3958 px, −1.05 %; 22/3 epithelial; 11 fibroblasts, 13
  myofibroblasts; `G` 0.4722, `C` 0.6258; 0 repairs, 9 divisions; integrity 0/0/0), and all 258
  compared per-MCS log lines identical (field `G`/`C` stats every 100 MCS, lumen area, sheet yCOM,
  every repair/mitosis/recruitment event). Declaring a sourceless field changes nothing.
- **`P`-on vs `P`-off, same seed: identical on every B4 column and all 258 log lines** — as it must be,
  since `P` has no consumer yet and the `P` code makes no `random` draw.
- **`P` field, 3k MCS (first smoke run):** band mean 0.26 at 500, 0.47 at 1000, 0.72 at 2000, **0.845 at
  3000**; min 0.73 (outer boundary), max 1.115 (at the sheet); smooth, no overshoot, no NaN. The field
  is 87 % of the way to steady state at 3k (1 − e^−3000k), so the projected steady band mean is **≈ 0.97
  against the design 1.0**. `lumen_P_mean` = 0.977 (> band mean: the lumen is 10–30 px from the basal
  source and a dead-end reservoir; the band extends 67 px below it). `P_analogue_H5` = 0.1394
  (= ⟨WS⟩(1 + f_d) as expected). `S_M1` at a pulse MCS ≈ 3.2e-4 per band pixel vs the epithelial
  per-pixel writes of order 1e-2; the pedestal is the ODE's 15 % share by construction. Source
  fallbacks: 2 before MCS 1500, 1268 by 3000 (myofibroblasts covering some cells' basal sides from
  ~MCS 1800 on; the fallback writes into the cell's own basal row, so no `P` is lost).
- **Wall time, 3k MCS, runs alone, after the two fixes:** switches off 303 s; `P` on 333 s
  (**+30 s, +10 %**; extrapolated to a 20k run, ≈ +3.3 min on ~55 min). First, concurrent runs before the fixes: off 467 s, on 752 s, with the pedestal loop
  and the per-MCS band mean each about half the excess. Steppable split of the +30 s: `ODESignalSteppable` +20 s (the band pedestal, 6 of every 24 MCS),
  `FibrosisModelSteppable` +11 s (source writes, `local_P` reads), `DiagnosticsSteppable` +0.5 s; C++ time
  unchanged at ~71 s (the 20 solver sub-steps for `P` are already paid in the off run, since the field is
  declared there too). The re-run after the fixes reproduced the first run's `P` readouts exactly (0.8446 /
  0.9774 / 0.1394), so the `WS` floor drops nothing measurable; the fallback count fell to 364 because
  between-pulse MCS are no longer counted.
  Budget in the plan §3 was ~3× the `S_M2` pedestal cost for all three new loops; `P` alone is within it.
