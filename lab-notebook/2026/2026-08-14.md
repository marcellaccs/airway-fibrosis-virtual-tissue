## Decisions
- **Lumen-scoped diagnostics should always derive from the same flood-fill, not
  independent fixed-y checks.** The bug fixed today was the second instance of this class of
  problem (`compute_lumen_area` itself was already flood-fill-based, precisely to avoid the
  y-cutoff failure mode the `field C lumen max` check still had). Recorded as the governing
  principle in the new `flood_fill_lumen_pixels` docstring so future diagnostics default to
  reusing it rather than re-deriving a fixed-band check.
- **Stay on the field-based ECM representation for v1** (see
  `docs/ecm_field_vs_celltype_assessment.md` for the full reasoning). The cell-type route's
  real advantages — ECM persisting independent of its producer cell, contact-area-gated state
  transitions, explicit MMP-mediated degradation, fiber-guided chemotaxis — aren't things v1's
  stated claim (Higham et al. lumen-narrowing target) currently needs. The open question
  is calibration of the existing field-to-`targetVolume` coupling, not the representation
  itself. Not a final decision — explicitly gated on today's full-run result: if `K_C`/
  `MYOF_TARGET_VOLUME_GROWTH_FACTOR` calibration can't reach the ~22% narrowing target without
  going non-physical, the cell-type route is the fallback `cc3d_v1_todo.md` PRIORITY 0 plan
  step 5 already anticipated, with Chandrasegaran et al. now confirmed as a usable reference
  implementation rather than a from-scratch design.

## Results / observations
- Smoke test (2000 MCS): clean, `fragmented=0 isolated=0` at MCS 0/1000/1999, flood-filled
  `field C lumen max` correctly 0 throughout even as the sheet pushed past `LUMEN_MIN_Y`.
- **Full 20,000-MCS confirmation complete, clean.** `fragmented=0 isolated=0` at MCS 0, 1000,
  19999; mesenchymal cells with COM inside epithelial band 0 at every checkpoint (0, 1000,
  19999) — cleaner than the `v1-fpp-stability20k` run, which had 1 at MCS 19999.
- **Flood-filled `field C lumen max` was exactly 0 at all 201 checkpoints (every 100 MCS,
  MCS 0-19999), no exceptions.** This confirms the diagnostic-artifact read from
  2026-08-13: the ~89-peak signal in `v1-fpp-stability20k-run.log` was entirely a fixed-y
  false positive from collagen sitting under displaced-but-intact epithelium, not real
  leakage. With the check correctly scoped to the flood-filled lumen, there is zero evidence
  of collagen ever reaching the open airway in this run, at any point.
- **Displacement was smaller and plateaued earlier than the previous stochastic run.**
  Sheet max yCOM only reached 80.25 by the run's end (barely past `LUMEN_MIN_Y=80`, and only
  briefly — most of the run sat at 79.73), vs. 83.08 in `v1-fpp-stability20k`. Lumen area
  narrowed to only -2.5% by MCS 19999, and most of that narrowing happened by MCS ~300-400
  (-2.1%) and barely moved for the remaining ~19,600 MCS — a fast early plateau, not
  progressive narrowing. Both runs used identical committed parameters (`K_C=20`,
  `MYOF_TARGET_VOLUME_GROWTH_FACTOR=2.0`); the gap (-2.5% vs -5.5%) is run-to-run stochastic
  variance in an unseeded CPM run, not a parameter difference — itself worth noting as a
  reproducibility concern for whatever value gets used to judge the Higham ~22% target.
- **Bottom line for the field-vs-cell-type question:** both runs are well short of the ~22%
  narrowing target (2.5-5.5% vs. 22%), and the mechanism plateaus early rather than
  progressing — this looks like a calibration ceiling, not measurement noise, now that the
  diagnostic is trustworthy. Doesn't yet call for abandoning the field representation (still
  only one calibration point, `K_C=20`/growth factor `2.0`, tested), but the "if calibration
  can't get there" condition in `docs/ecm_field_vs_celltype_assessment.md`'s recommendation
  is closer than it looked before this run.
