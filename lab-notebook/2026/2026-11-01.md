## Decisions

- **Same D for `G` and `P`, difference carried by decay** (Marcella). Basis: TGF-β1 (25 kDa dimer) and IL-6
  (21–26 kDa) are the same molecular size, so free D is nearly identical by Stokes–Einstein; what differs is
  decay (TGF-β latent and matrix-bound; IL-6 soluble, short-lived). A 44× D gap on one pin would produce
  effects with no comparative basis. The literature pass turns the pin into a stated range for a Gate D
  sensitivity axis (roughly λ at 3, 12, 80 px).
- Lumen `P`: accepted as a readout (above). Decision item 5 closed.
- The 10-31 `P`-on results (D = 4.44) stay in the 10-31 notebook as the sensitivity-arm reference.

## Literature session result (same day, `2026-11-01_lit_diffusion_ranges.md`)

- 21 peer-reviewed sources (dossier [78]–[98], reviewer notes 16–20), five rows in the pins table, five
  §7 anchors, 21 bib entries (130 → 151), four figures flagged *(verify)* pending full text. Its five
  calibration-table rows were appended before my `dc730c8` commit and went in with it (the message
  does not mention them); its other files (dossier, `validation_targets.md`, `citation_todo.md`,
  `references.bib`) are uncommitted, alongside Marcella's earlier 14 bib entries.
- **Finding:** free D is the same for TGF-β1 and IL-6 (84–128 µm²/s, `Young1980`, `Ross2018`) and
  unusable for either on this lattice (λ > 600 px); binding, not tortuosity, is what brings the range
  to cell scale, so the model's D is a binding-dressed value and the citable quantity is λ. `G`: ≤ 70 µm
  (`VanMeerbeeck2025`, verify) down to contact-only (`Campbell2020`, `Jin2024`). `P`: 25–120 µm with a
  250 µm ceiling (IL-8 is GAG-immobilised in lung, `Frevert2003`; IL-6 only partly retained,
  `Ramsden1992`). **The shared D = 0.10 chosen this morning puts both fields below their ranges**
  (λ_G = 4 µm, λ_P = 15 µm); it is defensible as the contact-only lower arm, not as the centre.
- Shared D is supported for free D, not for tissue-effective D (binding differs), but holds as a ratio:
  λ_P/λ_G = √(k_G/k_P) = 3.8 is inside the 1–10 the sources allow. If the one-knob rule is kept,
  D = 2.5–4.5 puts both in range (the 10-30 `P` pin of 4.44 already was, for `P`).
- `G` decay: active t½ 1.6–3 min in plasma, latent > 100 min; k_G bounded 0.0072–0.45 /MCS; the current
  0.01 is the latent end and holds only if `G` is the lumped active + latent pool.
- Folded into: calibration table `G` diffusion and decay rows (status free → anchored as a range /
  bounded) and the `P` diffusion row status; plan Phase 1 status note (Gate D sensitivity axis, new
  decision item 6).
