### Decisions / flags for Marcella

- Plan §5 conversion error: 1–3 days is 1385–4154 MCS at 62.4 s/MCS, not 1440–4320.
- `P` will be near-uniform over the band at any defensible λ; consumers 1 and 3 see band-level `P`.
- `k_P` and `k_prolif` signs hold only if `P` is the exposure milieu, not IL-6 alone.
- The activation rate multiplies `G` in code; the per-MCS value must be divided by the `G` that means
  saturating TGF-β1 before it goes into `ACTIVATION_RATE_PER_MCS`.
