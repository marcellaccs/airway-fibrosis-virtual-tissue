## Results / observations

- **The RBM anchor confirms the layout and then immediately fails as a readout.** Healthy RBM ≈ 4.7 µm
  is 3.8 px at 1.25 µm/px; COPD ≈ 6.0 µm is 4.8 px. The entire disease effect is **one pixel wide**.
  So RBM thickness is a good check that the subepithelial band is physically sized, and it is not a
  quantity this lattice can report. This is a resolution limit, not a calibration limit, and A18's
  ordinal-validation stance does not cover it — worth stating before a reviewer finds it.
- **The wood-smoke literature argues against the model's current exposure coupling.**
  `RecillasRoman2021`'s growth-rate response is **trimodal**: 0.5–1% WSE increases growth rate, 2.5%
  decreases it *without* cell damage, 5–20% inhibits and damages — and the ECM-signaling peak
  (TGF-β1, Col I/III, MMP-1, TIMP-1/2 all up) sits at 2.5%, a dose that *reduces* proliferation. On
  migration, everything found points the same, opposite-to-model direction: biomass particles leave
  A549 motility unchanged to 125 µg/mL and reduce migration and motility speed at 250–500
  (`Albuquerque2021`); cigarette-smoke proxies inhibit fibroblast migration via adhesion/cytoskeletal
  change (`Wong2004`) and via ROS/AMPK (`Shin2019`). A single monotonic "exposure severity" scalar
  driving both proliferation and motility upward cannot reproduce either limb.
- **`Zajicek1992` also gives a turnover ordering worth keeping** for the new GF-gated mitosis rule:
  labeling index 1.24 ± 0.1 (fibroblast) vs 0.13 ± 0.02 (epithelial) at 1 h — fibroblasts turn over
  roughly 10× faster than epithelium at baseline in normal bronchus.
- Both new cell-count numbers are abstract-level and marked *(verify)*; `Zajicek1992` reports SE, not
  SD, and is hamster.
