## Decisions
- **A19 is a standalone numbered assumption, not an expansion of A14.** A14's stationary-source
  bullet asserts only that injured epithelium *is* a growth-factor source. The load-bearing claim
  is the *differential* — damaged secretes more than healthy — because that is what imprints the
  damage pattern on the field and makes the focal-vs-diffuse experiment (A14, A18) meaningful. Left
  implicit inside A14 it would not get challenged, and it is exactly the kind of claim a reviewer
  presses on. A14 now cross-references A19 rather than restating it.
- **The citable quantity is a ratio with a non-zero healthy floor, not an absolute rate.** No source
  found gives a calibrated per-cell secretion rate for small-airway epithelium, and A7 forbids
  absolute units anyway. A19 therefore states the damaged:healthy ratio as a **scanned parameter**
  and says so explicitly, rather than implying the literature pins it down. This also slots cleanly
  into the A14 `S_M2` scan: `S_M2` sets how close the wall is to tipping, the ratio sets how sharply
  the damage pattern is imprinted.
- **Kept the healthy rate non-zero.** The current CC3D draft already secretes from both epithelial
  types; López-Guisa et al. (2012) measure a *difference* between asthmatic and healthy epithelial
  cells, not presence versus absence, so a zero healthy floor would overstate the evidence.
- **Recorded three challenges, not one.** House format in the dossier is supports-plus-challenges;
  the three are substantively different and each maps to a different part of the model:
  (a) Yao et al. (2021) — signalling is bidirectional and v1 omits the fibroblast→epithelium return
  arm (same caveat A1 already carries via You et al., 2023); (b) Di Vincenzo et al. (2022) and
  Daud et al. (2025) — "damaged" is not monotonically "secreting more," and *shed* epithelium
  secretes less, whereas v1's damaged cells persist and keep secreting; (c) Kraik et al. (2024) —
  TGF-β is initially protective in COPD, so A19's direction holds only inside the persistent-exposure
  regime A5 and A11 already scope.
- **Framed A19 as paracrine signalling only**, explicitly consistent with A17: epithelium is a
  growth-factor *source*, not a cell source of myofibroblasts. That is the role the lineage-tracing
  literature supports (Hung, 2020), and it is why A19 can be asserted strongly while EMT stays a v2
  item.
- **`Checa2016` (cigarette-smoke extract) and `Baarsma2022` (diesel exhaust particles) are proxies,
  not wood-smoke evidence.** Named as such in A19 and logged as an open to-do rather than quietly
  cited as if exposure-matched.
- **Did not hand-edit `literature/references.bib`.** `CLAUDE.md` treats it as a Zotero export, so
  hand-added entries would be overwritten at the next export. The nine papers are logged in
  `citation_todo.md` for the identifier-import route instead.

## Results / observations
- The direction of A19 is better supported than expected, at three distinct levels of evidence:
  - **Association with the lesion** — Kumar et al. (2004): epithelial expression of the cleaved,
    potentially bioactive TGF-β1 rose with allergen challenge and its accumulation was related to
    airway-wall remodeling; eosinophils were *not* a significant source, which isolates the
    epithelium as the relevant stationary source rather than merely one of many.
  - **A like-for-like healthy-vs-diseased comparison at the cell level** — López-Guisa et al.
    (2012): epithelial cells brushed from asthmatic children (n = 16) vs healthy children (n = 15),
    differentiated 3 weeks at air–liquid interface, produced significantly more TGF-β2 and VEGF and
    ~3.7-fold more periostin. The difference survives removal from the airway, so it is a property
    of the epithelial cells themselves. This is the closest analogue in the literature to a
    two-cell-type secretion-rate difference and is the single most useful paper found.
  - **Direct exposure and causal necessity** — Checa et al. (2016): cigarette-smoke extract raised
    secretion of *activated* TGF-β1 (integrin-αv-dependent) versus unexposed controls;
    Baarsma et al. (2022): diesel exhaust particles raised TGF-β1 protein in bronchial epithelial
    cells; Yang et al. (2014): lung-epithelial-specific CTGF deletion attenuated bleomycin fibrosis
    in vivo, so epithelium-derived growth factor is load-bearing, not incidental.
- Checa et al. (2016) is a bonus for **A14**, not just A19: the increase is in *activated* TGF-β1
  and is integrin-αv-dependent, which is direct support for A14's latent-vs-active separation and
  for the stiffness/integrin activation feedback flagged there for v2.
- The gap that remains is quantitative, and it is the same shape as A1's: the literature supports
  the *direction* firmly and supplies no *number*. Handled the same way A1 was — state it as a
  modeling posit and a scanned parameter rather than asserting a value.
- No exposure-matched (wood-smoke or biomass PM) paper was found for the epithelial-secretion
  differential specifically. `Zou2014` and `Li2024`, already in the docs, cover wood-smoke small-airway
  remodeling but not epithelial growth-factor output per se.
