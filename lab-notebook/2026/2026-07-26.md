## Decisions
- **Name the v1 model for the lesion, not the disease.** Working title:
  *A virtual tissue model of subepithelial fibrosis in the small airway*, subtitled
  "the initiating lesion of biomass-smoke small airway remodeling."
- **"Small airway remodeling" is retained in the subtitle, not dropped.** It is the
  established term in the wood-smoke literature specifically (Zou et al., 2014;
  Zhao et al., 2018), which reverses an earlier concern that it over-claimed.
- **Do not claim airflow limitation or a resistance readout.** Lumen cross-sectional area is
  plotted as geometry only, not converted to resistance. Polosukhin et al. (2021) found loss
  of radial alveolar attachments, not wall thickening, to be the major determinant of
  airflow limitation in small airways.
- **Drop the smooth-muscle layer for v1**; replace with a non-deformable outer boundary that
  supplies the inward-growth bias and occupies the slot ASM would later fill.
- **Stay dimensionless.** Report collagen area fraction and relative lumen area; no
  lattice-site-to-µm or MCS-to-hours mapping in v1. This removes the scale-calibration chain
  that a Wa/Pbm or resistance readout would have required.
- **ECM deposits subepithelially, not into the lumen** — supersedes the earlier design note
  in `Model_Notes_digitized.md` §B.7.
- **No neutrophils in v1**, now positively justified rather than merely simplified:
  biomass COPD shows reduced neutrophil infiltration vs. cigarette COPD (Zhao et al., 2018).
- **Primary endpoint is collagen area fraction**, matching Islam et al. (2023) and
  Brown et al. (2011) and comparable to human small-airway histology (Eapen et al., 2021).

## Session 2 — tissue-layer scope correction and the ODE coupling scheme

PI clarified that macrophages will **not** be represented in the tissue layer (they stay in the
ODE), epithelium is stationary, and fibroblasts/myofibroblasts are the mobile cells — so the
stationary growth-factor source is damaged epithelium and the mobile sources are the mesenchymal
cells. Macrophage TGF-β enters the field from the ODE under an averaging assumption.

### Decisions (session 2)
- **A13 — macrophages live only in the ODE layer.** The ODE/tissue split is by timescale and
  mixing (fast well-mixed immune vs. slow spatially structured mesenchymal), which makes it
  principled rather than a convenience. Consequence: **granuloma is retired from scope**, and the
  old M1→M2 CC3D prototype is no longer a direct precursor to the tissue layer (it still
  demonstrates CC3D mechanics, so the feasibility slide survives, but the "scaling up" phrasing
  overstates continuity).
- **A11 rewritten — cycle-averaging with drift, not strict QSS.** Strict quasi-steady state sends
  M2 to a constant, which would give one static lesion and no progression. The published ODE under
  recurring exposure gives diminishing M1 peaks with *progressive M2 accumulation* — a limit cycle
  with a rising baseline. Correct reduction: constant **within** an exposure cycle, drifting upward
  **across** cycles.
- **A14 — macrophage contribution enters as a uniform additive pedestal `S_M2`, not a
  multiplicative gain.** A uniform source against uniform decay adds no gradient (hence no
  chemotactic force) but shifts the whole wall toward the differentiation threshold. That gives two
  orthogonal knobs: `S_M2` = how close the tissue is to tipping; epithelial damage pattern = where
  it tips first. Makes the §B.5 Anti/Pro phase space executable as a parameter scan and reduces the
  antioxidant experiment to a single scalar link. Multiplicative gain deferred to v2.
- **A15 — the ODE→tissue interface is exactly two slowly drifting scalars:** `S_M2` (uniform GF
  source) and `R_fib` (fibroblast recruitment at the boundary). Recruitment included because
  subepithelial thickening tracks fibroblast *number* (Hoshino et al., 1998, rs = 0.88), so a fixed
  seed pool that only differentiates will under-produce thickening.
- **A16 — layer order was wrong in the original schematic.** Correct order outer→inner is
  boundary / subepithelial compartment / epithelium / lumen. The original put the mobile cell layer
  between epithelium and lumen, i.e. fibroblasts on the luminal side of the epithelium. Same error
  as A9, expressed geometrically.
- **A17 — EMT deferred to v2, and the spatial pro-inflammatory field with it.** Nothing in the v1
  tissue responds to P, so a spatial P field is dead weight; P stays an ODE scalar. EMT is the
  designated v2 addition because it gives P a job and is the strongest CPM-specific mechanism
  available (loss of adhesion + gain of motility), i.e. the best answer to "why CompuCell3D rather
  than a simpler framework."

### Observations (session 2)
- **Known failure mode, to be treated as a result.** Since `S_M2` drifts upward it will eventually
  exceed the differentiation threshold alone, converting all fibroblasts synchronously and erasing
  spatial structure — at which point the spatial layer earns nothing over the ODE. The `S_M2` value
  where the lesion goes **focal → diffuse** is the threshold to locate deliberately, and it
  operationalizes "when does remodeling become self-sustaining" from slide 4. This is now the
  headline v1 experiment.
- **Positioning vs. Islam et al. (2023) improves rather than weakens.** Homogenizing the macrophage
  source is the complementary experiment to theirs: stripping macrophage sources of spatial
  structure isolates whether epithelial patterning *alone* produces a focal lesion — a variable
  Islam confounded, since they identified M2 as the primary contributor to collagen area fraction.
- **Implementation constraints noted:** the GF field needs an explicit decay term (uniform source
  with no decay accumulates without bound); lateral boundaries must be no-flux (Neumann), since a
  fixed-value boundary against a uniform source creates a spurious edge gradient that drags
  fibroblasts sideways.
- **Extension over Brown et al. (2011) clarified.** Same variable list, but three structural
  differences: the lesion is compartmentalized rather than aggregate; M1/M2 are distinct
  spatially-distributed states so the A/P ratio emerges from local population composition rather
  than being a global scalar; and the signal layer is calibrated to our own RAW 264.7 wood-smoke
  ELISA data rather than abstracted. The novel *question* is focal vs. diffuse injury at matched
  total damage, which Brown's abstracted patch cannot pose.

## Results / observations
- **Closest prior art validates the minimal set.** Brown et al. (2011) built an ABM of
  particulate-exposure lung inflammation and fibrosis using only macrophages, fibroblasts,
  TNF-α, TGF-β1, collagen and tissue damage, and produced three emergent states validated
  against mouse histology. Smooth muscle, MMPs and a basement-membrane object are not
  required for a publishable result.
- **Timescale separation is the real technical risk (A11).** Li et al. (2024) found biomass
  PM2.5 produced small-airway inflammation with *no* structural change at 4 weeks and
  remodeling only at 24 weeks; Zou et al. (2014) needed 4–7 months of wood smoke. The
  published ODE model runs over hours to days. The tissue layer must therefore be driven by
  a time-averaged or per-exposure-cycle summary of ODE output, not instantaneous coupling.
  This is not yet reflected anywhere in the CC3D design notes.
- **Human validation target identified.** Eapen et al. (2021) report α-SMA⁺ myofibroblast
  density per mm² of lamina propria, lamina propria thickness, and collagen-1/fibronectin
  change in COPD small airways, correlating with lung-function decline — the same quantities
  the model produces natively, requiring no new geometry.
- **Caveats to hold in reserve:** Morán-Mendoza et al. (2008) found wood-smoke lung disease
  histopathologically comparable to smokers with interstitial fibrosis absent; Higham et al.
  (2022) found collagen *decreased* in severe COPD airway walls, so ECM burden is not
  monotone with disease stage.
- **Highest-value reusable artifact** of the v1 build is the ODE-to-field coupling steppable,
  not the biology. Cell types, fields and the collagen readout port forward unchanged.
