## Decisions

None finalised. The doc is a proposal. Positions taken in it, for the record:

- **The placement rule for damaged cells is the focal-vs-diffuse experiment**, not an
  implementation detail. Both arms consume identical `f_d` from the ODE, so total injury is
  matched by construction and `spatial_stats.py` scores the difference. This is the tidiest
  version of the seeding idea in the notes.
- **Damage must be a persistent per-cell state, not a re-drawn random location set.** Resampling
  which cells are damaged each step makes the growth-factor source flicker and destroys focus
  identity. Track it as a cell state with transition probabilities tuned so the population mean
  follows `f_d`.
- **One-way coupling for v1**, with an explicit trigger for when two-way earns its cost: as soon
  as the tissue layer contains a mechanism that changes epithelial damage independently of the ODE
  (EMT, stiffness-driven stress, epithelial loss). Until then the returned `f_d` is a resampled
  copy of the ODE's own `Ed`, and overwriting `Ed` from the slow layer breaks the A11 averaging
  argument.
- **Do not use raw `A/P` as the scaling factor.** `P` decays to near zero between meals
  (τ = 1/`m7` = 25 h) while `A` ratchets up, so the ratio is unbounded and spikes on the *gaps* in
  the exposure schedule rather than on the exposure. Use `A/(A+P+ε)` or a Hill function of
  cycle-averaged `A`; recommend the Hill form, since a non-monotone driver makes the
  focal-to-diffuse threshold uninterpretable.
- **Carry the additive pedestal and the multiplicative gain in the same field equation** rather
  than choosing. `∂G/∂t = D∇²G − λG + S_M2 + ρ·(k_E·1[damaged] + k_F·1[fibroblast])` recovers
  either pure arm by setting one term to zero. A14 currently commits to additive for v1 and calls
  multiplicative the v2 refinement; the extra cost of carrying both is one multiplication, and it
  converts the commitment into a measurable.
- **Fibroblast recruitment: keep `R_fib` scalar, bias only the placement by local `G`.** This
  gives spatially focal recruitment with no second PDE and leaves the A15 interface at exactly two
  scalars, so the "reusable artifact" claim survives. A separate chemokine/PDGF field is the v2
  version and is the natural home for the bidirectional fibroblast–macrophage loop.
- **Precomputed CSV signal table for v1, live SBML as the v2 path.** Route 1 never installs
  Tellurium into the `cc3d` env, which `CLAUDE.md` warns can break libRoadRunner. Write the
  steppable against a `get_signal(cycle)` accessor so the swap is one function.

## Results / observations

### Correction: the repair-term "bug" is real but benign

Session 1 flagged the M2/A-modulated repair term as a blocking bug and claimed `f_d` was
untrustworthy. **That was wrong, and MMT was right to push back.** Corrected after writing
`model/ode/repair_term_check.py` and actually integrating it.

- What is genuinely inconsistent: `Ed'` uses `e4*(1 + e8*(M2+M2R) + e9*A)*Ed`, matching the
  declared comments (`e8` = M2 enhancement, `e9` = A enhancement). `Eh'` uses
  `e4*(1 + e9*(M2+M2R) + e10*A)*Ed` in all three full-model cells, i.e. the older numbering where
  `e9` = M2 and `e10` = A. The `Eh'` line was never renumbered when `e8` was inserted ahead of it.
- **Cell 23 (Figure 18, antioxidant scans) is fully consistent** — old numbering on both lines,
  `e8` left at 0.0 and unused, scan code sets `r.e9`/`r.e10` to match.
- **The numerical effect is negligible.** `f_d` differs by 0.21% at `t` = 200 and 0.00% at
  `t` = 4000; `Eh+Ed` by 0.5–0.7%. At the corners of the Figure 22 LHS ranges (`e8`, `e9`
  sampled independently over 1–100), worst case is 1.8% on `Eh+Ed` and **0.04% on `f_d`**.
- Why: `Ed'` is the correct line, `Ed` sits at `≈ e3·WS·Eh/(e2 + e4·out_fac)`, the mismatch
  perturbs `Eh` only, and the logistic proliferation term absorbs it — `Eh` and `Ed` shift
  together so the ratio is preserved. `f_d` is invariant almost by construction.
- **Published results stand. `f_d` is safe to use as published.** Renumbering `Eh'` is a
  readability tidy-up, not a correction.
- The two genuine errors are in the working notebooks, not the paper: undeclared `e8`/`e9`/`e10`
  in `Full model.ipynb` cells 4, 6, 7, and an unparseable duplicated fragment in
  `Full model parameter scans.ipynb` cell 2. Both should be deleted or marked superseded;
  `supplementary.ipynb` is the copy of record and cell 21 is the canonical full model.

### The signal saturates. It does not drift.

Integrating `supplementary.ipynb` cell 21 to `t` = 4000 h (~24 weeks) under the published
three-meals-a-day schedule, cycle means:

| cycle (day) | `f_d` | `M2_tot` | `A` | `A/(A+P)` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0.0369 | 0.1998 | 0.342 | 0.227 |
| 2 | 0.0609 | 0.4995 | 0.970 | 0.645 |
| 7 | 0.0858 | 0.4528 | 0.956 | 0.714 |
| 27 | 0.1113 | 0.4464 | 0.950 | 0.694 |
| 83 | 0.1133 | 0.4464 | 0.950 | 0.692 |
| 165 | 0.1133 | 0.4464 | 0.950 | 0.692 |

`M2_tot` and `A` overshoot near day 2 and are flat to five decimals from about day 27; `f_d`
settles by day 83 on the epithelial timescale (`1/e4` ≈ 21 days). Second half of the run:
`M2_tot` −0.28%, `f_d` −0.65%.

- Structural, not a parameter accident: the schedule is exactly periodic and **no ODE state
  variable ratchets** — every species is a birth–death balance relaxing to a periodic attractor.
  The published 200 h window shows only the rising limb, which is why A11 read it as progressive
  accumulation.
- **A11 is no longer the principal technical risk.** There is no drift to homogenize; cycle-average
  once at the attractor.
- **The ODE→tissue interface is a constant per scenario, not a time series** — three numbers per
  exposure/treatment condition. The signal table becomes a handful of rows.
- **A14's drift-to-threshold failure mode cannot occur.** The focal→diffuse threshold has to be
  found by scanning `S_M2` across scenarios rather than by running longer. Better experiment.
- **The collagen field is the only ratchet in the coupled system.** Progression must come from the
  ECM field integrating a sustained source. Independent argument for A14's burden-not-density call.

### The antioxidant intervention moves the two channels in opposite directions

Cell 23 scaling, run to the attractor:

| condition | `f_d` | `M2_tot` | `A` | `A/(A+P)` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| baseline | 0.1141 | 0.4558 | 0.955 | 0.684 |
| 5× down / 5× up | 0.0200 | 0.5363 | 2.434 | 0.974 |
| 10× / 10× | 0.0068 | 0.6062 | 3.894 | 0.992 |
| 20× / 20× | 0.0012 | 0.6252 | 6.786 | 0.998 |

Damage collapses ~90×, but `M2_tot` rises 37% and `A` sevenfold. **The interface choice sets the
sign of the antioxidant result**: damage seeding says strongly protective, an `S_M2` pedestal says
mildly profibrotic, a Hill gain on `A` says strongly profibrotic. This is the framing mismatch
between the two models made numerical — in the ODE the endpoint is resolution and repair, so more
A/M2 reads as recovery; in a fibrosis model it is the pathogenic direction. Partly by construction,
since the intervention is *defined* as scaling anti-inflammatory production and repair enhancement
up. Written into the antioxidant assumption with three ways out.

### Other

- **The ODE time unit is hours** — `WS` events key on `time % 24`, meal windows 8–9, 13–14, 18–19.
  Published runs stop at `t` = 200, about 8 days.
- **Schedule quirk:** no event covers `0 <= time % 24 < 8`, so `WS` keeps its initial 1.0 for the
  first 8 hours of the run. Figure 17 opens with an unintended 8-hour continuous exposure before
  the three-meal pattern begins. Affects the transient only, not the attractor.
- Stiffness was not a problem in practice: RK4 at `dt` = 0.005 is converged (changing `dt` 4×
  moves `f_d` by 7e-7), despite the `m1` = 50 vs `m4` = 0.02 spread and discontinuous `WS`.
- `repair_term_check.py` runs in the sandbox with **no tellurium and no scipy**, so interface
  sweeps are possible here without touching `cc3d` or `tox-ode`. It is an independent
  reimplementation, which is a check but not a proof — confirm against Tellurium before publishing.
- The old M1/M2 prototype steppable is more useful than A13 implies. It does not port forward as
  biology, but it is a working demonstration of the live-SBML route (`add_free_floating_antimony`,
  `step_size`, `timestep_sbml`), so Route 2 is a proven fallback rather than a gamble. Its
  `Tellurium.xml` is bare (200×200, no cell types beyond Medium, no fields), so nothing there
  substitutes for the v1 geometry.
- Sandbox still has no package network, so none of the ODE runs above could be executed here; all
  findings are from reading the model strings, not from simulation.
