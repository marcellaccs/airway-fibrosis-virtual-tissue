## The finding that reorders the plan

**The v2 component on the critical path is the spatial pro-inflammatory field `P`, not the
volume-occupying ECM cell type.**

`model_spec_v1_v2.md` §v2.2 lists `P` as "promoted from an ODE scalar once EMT gives it a target."
That gating condition is obsolete. `P` has a target now, for four independent reasons:

1. The Frontiers topic is epithelial–immune crosstalk; `P` is the immune-facing species.
2. The ODE already emits it, validated against Calcabrini (2004).
3. Bennet et al. report IL-6/IL-8 as their only statistically significant exposure response, and
   the model currently emits nothing from their assay panel.
4. A19a (from the 2026-10-23 correction) is a claim about pro-inflammatory secretion, and the
   tissue layer has no `P` to carry it.

The 2026-09-06 decision prioritizing the volume-occupying ECM cell type is not wrong — it remains
the right answer to the displacement gap. But the displacement gap is not on the critical path to
*this* paper: lumen narrowing is Tier 1 in `validation_targets.md` §3.5, a sanity check. The paper
does not need to reach −22%.
