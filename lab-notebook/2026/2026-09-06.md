- **Fibroblast mitosis built** (`FibroblastMitosisSteppable`,
  `FibrosisModelSteppable.update_fibroblast_mitosis`,
  `Subepithelial_FibrosisSteppables.py`). Standard CC3D volume-doubling mitosis
  (`MitosisSteppableBase`, pattern confirmed against `.claude/skills/cc3d-expert`'s
  `references/steppable-patterns.md`): a fibroblast's `mitosis_clock` accumulates only while local
  `G >= p.FIBROBLAST_MITOSIS_G_THRESHOLD` (paused, not reset, below it — a brief excursion out of
  a permissive region shouldn't erase progress), and once it reaches `p.FIBROBLAST_CYCLE_MCS`
  (~1730 MCS) the cell's `targetVolume` is set to 2x; `FibroblastMitosisSteppable.step()` watches
  for `cell.volume` to catch up to ~90% of that and performs the actual split via
  `divide_cell_random_orientation`, then `update_attributes()` halves `targetVolume` back down and
  resets both daughters' `dividing`/`mitosis_clock`/`activation_progress` state. Mitosis and
  differentiation (`activate_fibroblast`) are kept mutually exclusive per cell per step so they
  don't fight over the same cell's `targetVolume`.
  - `FIBROBLAST_MITOSIS_G_THRESHOLD = 0.5 * FIB_TO_MYOF_G_THRESHOLD` (0.225): no direct citation
    for a "remodeling region" concentration exists, so this is a scanned/uncalibrated parameter,
    same convention A19 already uses — biases mitosis toward the same active-signal regions as
    differentiation without being identical to that threshold.
  - `FIBROBLAST_CYCLE_MCS` is computed from `FIBROBLAST_CYCLE_TIME_H=30.0` (a round value within
    the cited 25-34h range, Mio1992/Holz2004) via `S_PER_MCS` — see below.

## Decisions
- Mitosis is a "build it because it's real" feature, not a lever for the displacement question —
  explicitly not calibrated against the Higham ~22% target, and explicitly not raised in rate to
  compensate for anything. If it turns out to meaningfully move the plateau anyway, that's a
  side-effect to note, not a design goal.
- v2's volume-occupying ECM cell type moves from PRIORITY 0 plan step 5's "last resort" framing to
  a prioritized v2 item — it's mechanistically closer to what actually thickens fibrotic tissue in
  vivo (matrix, not cell mass) than any cell-crowding-based v1 fix could be.
- s/MCS = 62.4 (IPF/IPF) is the working baseline going forward; every place it's cited also
  carries the conservative alternative and the etiology-mismatch limitation, so a future
  manuscript/reviewer sees the judgment call rather than a bare number.

- **Full 20,000-MCS confirmation run, against the actual committed project (not a scratch copy)
  — clean.** `results/cc3d/v1-mitosis-confirm20k/`, log at
  `results/cc3d/v1-mitosis-confirm20k-run.log`. No traceback, no assertion failures (epithelial
  count conservation held throughout), sheet integrity `fragmented=0 isolated=0` at MCS 0, 1000,
  and 20000, 0 mesenchymal cells with COM inside the epithelial band at every checkpoint. Runtime
  39m58s — comparable to pre-mitosis full runs, negligible added overhead.
  - **Mitosis total: 3 divisions over the full run** (MCS ~8000-9000, then none for the
    remaining 11,000 MCS). Much lower than the 7 divisions seen by MCS ~2000 in the shorter
    scratch checks — not a bug, an emergent consequence of the rates involved: differentiation
    (`FIB_TO_MYOF_G_THRESHOLD=0.45`, ~50 MCS to complete once qualifying) is much faster than
    mitosis (`FIBROBLAST_MITOSIS_G_THRESHOLD=0.225`, ~1730 MCS to complete), so a fibroblast
    sitting in a high-G region almost always differentiates long before it could ever divide.
    Only fibroblasts that spend sustained time in the narrower 0.225-0.45 G band — mostly
    available early in the run, before the ODE-driven `S_M2` pedestal pushes domain-wide G past
    both thresholds (mean G reached 2.7 by MCS 20000) — get the chance to complete a division.
    By MCS ~9000 essentially no fibroblast anywhere sees G below 0.45 for long, so new recruits
    (via `R_fib`) differentiate almost immediately instead. This is exactly the "reasonable/low
    level, not a lever" behavior the user asked for, and it emerged from the citations rather
    than needing to be tuned down by hand.
  - **Displacement: -4.6% lumen narrowing at MCS 20000** — within the same 2-7% range seen
    across prior stochastic runs at `FIBROBLAST_INITIAL_NUMBER=80` (2026-09-03), confirming
    mitosis at this level does not meaningfully move the displacement plateau, consistent with
    the design decision that it isn't meant to.
