## Addendum 2: Route 2 resolved — smooth `WS` fixes the numerical wall

**Focus:** the Route 2 blocker above (CVODE fails on discrete `WS` events, RK4 diverges to
NaN) — user proposed replacing the discrete on/off exposure events with a continuous
Heaviside-like (sigmoid) approximation. Implemented, hit a second numerical issue, fixed,
verified over a full run.

### Did
- Replaced the six `E1`-`E6` Antimony `at` events with a single continuous assignment rule
  (`WS := ` sum of three rising-minus-falling sigmoid pairs, one per meal, same `:=` syntax
  the model already uses for `RM1`/`RM2`). Written as one line — Antimony's parser does not
  accept a multi-line expression with leading `+`/`-` continuations (first attempt threw
  "syntax error, unexpected '+'").
- First steepness choice (`WS_STEEPNESS=50`) hit a *new* failure, `CV_TOO_MUCH_ACC` at t=4h
  (hours before any meal transition) — traced to `exp()` overflow: `time%24` ranges over 24,
  so `WS_STEEPNESS*24=1200` far exceeds double precision's ~709 overflow point. Each
  individual sigmoid term still evaluated cleanly to 0 or 1 (IEEE754 `1/(1+inf)=0` is
  well-defined), but the repeated `inf` intermediates apparently corrupted CVODE's internal
  Jacobian/step-size estimation regardless.
- Lowered to `WS_STEEPNESS=20` (`20*24=480`, safely under the overflow threshold). Clean
  1000-MCS smoke test: `M2_tot=0.4498` at MCS 500, within 0.8% of the reference attractor
  `0.4464` — well before full saturation, strong early confirmation.
- Restored `ODESignalSteppable` to drive the field from the live model (default adaptive
  CVODE integrator, no tolerance overrides needed once `WS` is smooth) and reran the full
  20000-MCS acceptance run.
- **Full run: clean.** 1750.9 s (29m11s). Sheet integrity `fragmented=0, isolated=0` at
  MCS 0, 1000, and 19999. Mesenchymal containment 0 throughout. No crash, no NaN, no
  assertion failure. `M2_tot` holds a **stable periodic band (0.4388-0.4558) from MCS 500
  through MCS 19500** — the same six sampled values repeat every 3000 MCS (an exact multiple
  of the 24h meal cycle), confirming a genuine periodic attractor over the full ~833
  simulated days, matching `docs/ode_tissue_coupling_options.md` §1's prediction from the
  original notebook analysis.
- Updated `docs/tellurium_connection_walkthrough.md` end-to-end to describe the working
  process (9 steps now, was 8) rather than the blocked one.

### Decisions
- **`WS_STEEPNESS = 20`**, not 50. Three-way tradeoff, not just "safer is better": higher
  steepness tracks the true 1h square pulse more closely but is stiffer to integrate; lower
  is easier to integrate but visibly widens/softens the pulse and shifts the delivered dose
  per meal; independent of both, `WS_STEEPNESS*24` must stay under the `exp()` overflow
  point (~709) regardless of biological reasoning. 20 gives a ~18-minute transition against
  a 1-hour pulse — softer than the originally-intended ~6 minutes at steepness 50, but
  numerically clean, which is the constraint that actually bound the choice.
- **The smoothed `WS` is a real deviation from the published model**, not a numbering
  tidy-up like the `Eh'`/`Ed'` choice. Flagged explicitly in the walkthrough doc's Open
  Items — worth remembering if a result ever needs to match the discrete-event version.

## Addendum 3: Design consult — lumen representation and growth-factor/ECM source structure

**Focus:** two upstream modeling-design questions worked through with the assistant — (a) whether a
single lumped "growth factor" variable is adequate given TGF-β latency, and whether the
stationary-vs-mobile source distinction is worth keeping; (b) whether the lumen should be
CompuCell3D **Medium** or a frozen "airway" cell. Both resolved against A8/A9/A12/A14 and the
validation targets; the lumen decision is now a dated note under A8 in
`docs/model_assumptions_for_slides.md`.

### Did
- **Growth-factor / ECM sources.** Confirmed a single lumped *active* growth-factor field is
  adequate for v1, provided the activation term is kept separable so the latent pool and the
  stiffness-activation feedback can be gated on local ECM in v2. Islam et al. (2023) already varies
  stationary/mobile sources and latent/active TGF-β in an ABM, with collagen area fraction spanning
  2–40% — so these are first-order choices. Kept as the primary assumption (spatially structured
  sources) with three sub-elements and student attributions (one student each: mobile vs stationary;
  latent vs active; stiffness feedback).
- **Lumen representation.** Compared Medium vs a frozen airway cell and four candidate narrowing
  mechanisms in a table. Chose Option 1 (epithelial-sheet displacement); rejected Option 4
  (intraluminal ECM fill) against A9; deferred Option 3 (reticular-BM cell) to v2.
- Recorded the decision under **A8** and clarified **A14** (collagen field is non-diffusing) in
  `docs/model_assumptions_for_slides.md`; reworded A8 so the mechanism reads "subepithelial cell-mass
  expansion displaces the epithelial sheet luminally," not "ECM accumulation narrows the lumen."

### Decisions
- **Lumen = CompuCell3D Medium**, not a frozen airway cell — a frozen cell cannot be displaced, so
  the epithelial sheet could never move inward (lumen narrowing, `validation_targets.md` §3.5,
  unproducible) and it would turn `R_fib` recruitment into volume-constraint artefacts.
- **Narrowing = Option 1:** subepithelial cell mass (`R_fib`) displaces the intact epithelial sheet
  luminally against the frozen outer boundary; no cell crosses the epithelium. Couples lumen area to
  fibroblast number (Hoshino et al., 1998, rs = 0.88; Eapen et al., 2021), consistent with §3.2.
- **Collagen/ECM = non-diffusing accumulator field (D ≈ 0)**; only the growth-factor (TGF-β) field
  diffuses. This is consistent with the unbounded field-`C` growth already observed in the Group 1
  run above (that growth is the accumulator behaviour A14 specifies, gated later by Group 2's
  `C_norm`).
- Full reasoning, the rejected Option 4 and the deferred Option 3 are in the 2026-08-08 note under
  A8 of `docs/model_assumptions_for_slides.md`.

### Results / observations
- Consistency check passed: the lumen-narrowing output (validation §3.5; Higham et al. ~22%) is only
  producible with a displaceable (Medium) lumen, and the choice matches the Group 1 implementation
