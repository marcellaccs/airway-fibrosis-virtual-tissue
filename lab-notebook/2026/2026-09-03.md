## Did
Added four diagnostics to `DiagnosticsSteppable`
(`model/cc3d/subepithelial_fibrosis_draft/Simulation/Subepithelial_FibrosisSteppables.py`) and
ran each on a 3000-MCS scratch copy (`<scratchpad>/c_norm_diag_run`,
`fpp_off_diag_run`, `interface_occ_diag_run`, `vol_frustration_diag_run` — conda env
`cc3d_490`), testing hypotheses for the plateau in sequence, each ruling in/out the next:

1. **`print_myofibroblast_c_norm_stats`** (per-myofibroblast `local_C`/`c_norm(C)`
   distribution, at `C_NORM_CHECKPOINTS = (0,100,200,300,400,500,750,1000,2000,5000,10000)`).
   **Result: saturation ruled out.** `c_norm` mean was only 0.02 at MCS 300-400 (the plateau
   onset) and still just 0.17 by MCS 2999 — nowhere near the 0.9 threshold — while field `C`
   and the myofibroblast population both kept growing the whole run.
2. **FPP-link test**: same scratch setup, `EPITHELIAL_FPP_LAMBDA` dropped 50 → 0.
   **Result: not the primary cause.** Narrowing plateaued almost identically with links off
   (-2.7% to -4.1% vs. -3.1% to -3.4% with links on over the same MCS range) — links account
   for at most a small secondary drift, not the plateau itself. Sheet integrity stayed clean
   in both (`fragmented=0 isolated=0`).
3. **`print_interface_occupancy_stats`** (cell-pixel occupancy fraction in a 10-row band
   directly under the epithelium, `y=SUBEPITHELIAL_Y_MAX-9..SUBEPITHELIAL_Y_MAX`, vs. the
   whole subepithelial compartment). **Result: interface is not under-occupied relative to
   bulk — it's more crowded.** Interface occupancy climbed 14% (MCS 100) → 38% (MCS 2000),
   consistently higher than the bulk compartment's 15% → 22% over the same span, while lumen
   narrowing stayed flat at -2.1% to -2.4% the whole time.
4. **`print_volume_frustration_stats`** (`cell.volume`/`cell.targetVolume` ratio for
   interface-band myofibroblasts and for the epithelium). **Decisive result.** Interface
   myofibroblasts reach ~97-100% of their (growing) target volume throughout (mean 0.968-0.996
   across MCS 300-2999) — not volume-frustrated, i.e. not losing an energy competition against
   the sheet. The epithelium's own volume/targetVolume ratio sits at essentially exactly 1.000
   the entire run (0.979-1.021, CPM fluctuation noise only) — it is not being meaningfully
   compressed at all.

## Decisions
- **Conclusion: the plateau is not a gain, link-stiffness, or average-occupancy problem — the
  subepithelial compartment, even at its most crowded point directly under the sheet
  (~35-38% occupied), still has enough empty Medium pixels nearby for myofibroblasts to satisfy
  their `targetVolume` growth without ever needing to push against the epithelium.** The sheet
  isn't losing a fight it's currently in; it isn't in a fight yet. This confirms (rather than
  supersedes) the original PRIORITY 0 gap-1 diagnosis from before the `targetVolume` coupling
  landed ("subepithelial compartment nowhere near capacity") — the coupling fix (gap 2) made
  individual myofibroblasts grow with local `C`, but growth into abundant local slack isn't the
  same as growth that contests a boundary. The small narrowing that does happen (2-7% across
  today's and prior runs) looks like occasional stochastic pixel-copy wins at the sheet edge,
  not sustained mechanical pressure — consistent with plateauing fast and then only drifting.
- **This is the mechanistic case for fibroblast mitosis** (the other next-session item from
  2026-08-14): what's missing isn't more signal per cell, it's more cells, specifically enough
  to reach real confluence at the interface. Next session should scope GF-gated mitosis (or,
  as a cheaper first test, a substantially higher `R_FIB`) with this target explicitly in mind —
  interface occupancy needs to climb well past the ~38% ceiling seen today before the sheet
  would be expected to feel it.
- Ancillary: today's four 3000-MCS scratch runs plateaued at -2.1%, -3.1%/-4.1% (FPP on/off),
  and -6.8% respectively — a fourth-ish data point on top of the two full-run values already on
  record (2.5%, 5.5%), all under nominally identical parameters. The run-to-run spread looks
  real and fairly wide; still feeds the open plan step 3b (a second full 20,000-MCS replicate),
  not a substitute for it.

## Did (continued, same session)
User asked whether raw crowding (no mitosis, just a much higher initial fibroblast count)
would displace the sheet at all — a cheap test of the population-vs-signal conclusion above
before investing in mitosis design.

- **`FIBROBLAST_INITIAL_NUMBER` 80 → 300 scratch test** (3.75x baseline, same 3000-MCS scratch
  setup). Interface occupancy (10-row band) climbed to ~70% (vs. ~38% ceiling at 80), and
  narrowing landed at a fairly steady -7.8% by MCS 300-2999 — directionally more than the 2-7%
  stochastic range seen at 80 fibroblasts, but not dramatically so, and myofibroblast
  volume/targetVolume (~0.98) and epithelial volume/targetVolume (~1.000) still showed no
  frustration signature even at 70% aggregate occupancy. Suggestive, not conclusive on its own.
- **Added single-row boundary occupancy** to `print_interface_occupancy_stats` (y=73 only, the
  single row of pixels literally touching the epithelium at `EPITHELIAL_Y_MIN=74`), suspecting
  the 10-row average was diluting out the signal that actually matters. Re-ran the 300-fibroblast
  test with this added.
  - **Result: boundary-row occupancy peaks (72.5% at MCS 300) right when displacement is
    actively happening (-4.3% at MCS 100 → -10.4% at MCS 300), then declines steadily
    afterward (66.0% → 61.5% → 48.0% → 26.5% by MCS 2999) while narrowing stays locked at
    -10.6% to -10.9%.** Coherent mechanical story: a transient confluence spike at the single
    row touching the epithelium is what actually wins pixel-copy events and pushes the sheet;
    once won, `ConnectivityGlobal`/FPP hold the new position, so the boundary can de-densify
    afterward without the sheet retracting. This is a materially different (and more useful)
    signal than the 10-row band average, which stayed misleadingly flat (44-61%) and didn't
    track the actual displacement event at all.
  - This run's own narrowing (-10.6% to -10.9%) is a second 300-fibroblast replicate, above
    the first one's -7.8% — both above the 2-7% range at 80 fibroblasts, small-sample but
    consistent with crowding mattering.

## Decisions (continued)
- **Target metric for the mitosis/`R_fib` design going forward: boundary-row (single-row, not
  bulk) occupancy reaching a high transient peak.** A design that spreads population evenly
  through the compartment could look adequately "crowded" in aggregate while never actually
  spiking the one row of pixels that transmits pressure to the sheet. `print_interface_occupancy_stats`
  now reports this (`boundary row occupancy (y=SUBEPITHELIAL_Y_MAX only)`) alongside the
  existing 10-row and bulk numbers.
