## Decisions

- **ECM seeding (Marcella): seed small and ramp.** A new ECM cell is seeded on free Medium pixels
  only (2 × 2 where four are free at the chosen site, fewer otherwise, never overwriting a cell)
  with `targetVolume` = its seeded volume; the target then rises by `ECM_GROWTH_PX_PER_MCS`
  (first pass 0.5 px/MCS, full size in ≈ 65 MCS ≈ 1.1 h) until 36.
- Not built: 36 at birth (the plan's original wording), the 6 × 6 block drawn at once, a permanent
  deficit cap (it would stop the matrix from pressing on the sheet), and continuous deposition
  with the target tracking the myofibroblast's budget (cleaner accounting, more bookkeeping).
- **ECM removal (Marcella): rate-based, no threshold.** Each ECM cell is removed with probability
  `K_ECM_MMP · MMP_local` per MCS (`MMP_local` at its COM), placeholder `K_ECM_MMP` = 1e-4; the
  cell-based twin of the `C` MMP-decay term. A departure from the plan's §3 text and the borrowed
  Chandrasegaran threshold; the §2 criteria are untouched. Gate D scans `K_ECM_MMP` ∈ {0, 3e-5,
  1e-4, 3e-4, 1e-3} in place of `MMP_thr`.
- Not built: `MMP_thr` at the band mean or at the sheet-level value; a deterministic dose rule
  (same mean lifetime, no random stream, but synchronized removals and a form unlike the `C` term).
- **Myofibroblast `targetVolume` growth under ECM cells (Marcella): 0.**
  `MYOF_TARGET_VOLUME_GROWTH_FACTOR_WITH_ECM` = 0.0; λ_volume stiffening with `c_norm` stays. One
  control arm added to Gate D's sensitivity list (ECM off, swelling factor 0) because the ECM
  switch now changes two things. Recorded in plan §6 item 3, the todo and the calibration table.

## Results / observations

- Per pixel copy, volume term only: an ECM cell at 1 px with target 36 gains 10 · (2 · 35 − 1) = 690;
  at 30 px it gains 110; an epithelial cell loses 22.5 for its first pixel, 202 for its fifth, 427
  for its tenth. With amplitude 2 every downhill copy is accepted, so the unramped seedling fills
  out in a few MCS from whatever touches it, and placement is biased toward the sheet. The plan's
  "robust, no overwriting of neighbours" therefore does not hold for the full-target-at-birth form.
  Under the ramp the deficit stays near 1–2 px, a drive of 10–30, the scale of the contact energies.
- Long-run mechanics are unchanged by the ramp: the final target is 36, so a crowded band still
  builds pressure on the sheet, which is what the cell type is for.
- Caveat: fibroblast mitosis already makes a one-shot target jump (30 px at λ 8, a drive of ≈ 470)
  and the sheet has survived it, with one overlap flicker in the Phase 2 `P` + consumer 2 twin. ECM
  events will be about ten times more frequent and nearer the sheet. So the unramped form is a
  Tier 1 risk, not a certainty; the scratch comparison below settles it.
- Accounting consequence, accepted: the budget still drops by `ECM_QUANTUM` at creation, so a
  seedling deleted by MMP before it is full-grown costs a whole quantum.
- **Why a threshold fails here** (Phase 3 seed-200 3k numbers): the field is ≈ 5.8 · e^(−d/8 px)
  below the sheet (the fit reproduces the logged domain minimum, 0.0012 at the bottom of the band),
  so it spans three decades. MMP at the five sheet-adjacent myofibroblasts is 1.8–3.6, at the eight
  deep ones 0.000–0.005. A threshold at the band mean (0.7) is crossed at d ≈ 17 px: every ECM cell
  nearer the sheet is deleted at birth, none deeper is ever removed. The outcome is identical for
  any threshold from ≈ 0.005 to ≈ 1.8 (a plateau, then a cliff, for the Gate D scan), the cutoff
  drifts as the field rises with `P`, and the excluded zone is where subepithelial fibrosis is
  defined.
- **What the rate form gives at `K_ECM_MMP` = 1e-4:** mean ECM lifetime 1/(K · MMP) ≈ 3,300 MCS
  (2.4 days) at MMP = 3, ≈ 1,700 MCS at the source-pixel level of 6, effectively infinite deep in
  the band; steady count ≈ (1/450) · 3,300 ≈ 7 ECM cells per sheet-adjacent myofibroblast; a
  ramping seedling survives its ≈ 65 MCS with probability e^(−65 · 3e-4) ≈ 0.98. At `K_C_MMP`'s
  0.001 the lifetime (330 MCS) would be shorter than the 450-MCS deposition interval.
- Caveat: the near/deep split of myofibroblasts is one seed at 3k in the `G`-saturated regime that
  Phase 5's base-model change alters; the argument rests on the field's steepness, not the split.
- **What the swelling is worth** (Gate B4, `results/cc3d/v1-gateB-rebaseline20k/`, 20k, n = 6):
  42–62 myofibroblasts, mean `c_norm` at their COMs 0.08–0.20 (max 0.68–0.78, none ≥ 0.9), so mean
  extra target ≈ 30 · 2 · 0.11–0.20 ≈ 7–12 px per cell, ≈ 400–560 px over the band; bulk band
  occupancy 14–15 % (≈ 2,000 of 13,600 px), so swelling is ≈ 20 % of occupied volume. Lumen change
  −0.3 to −4.2 %. Under MMP + `C` term the swelling near the sheet is already nearly gone (mean
  `c_norm` 0.019 vs 0.085 at 3k).
- **Flag: the ECM budget against the myofibroblast count.** B4 seed 200 has 26 / 47 / 59
  myofibroblasts at 5k / 10k / 20k, ≈ 745,000 myofibroblast-MCS; at `ECM_BUDGET_INCREMENT` = 0.08
  px/MCS that is ≈ 60,000 px of matrix against ≈ 11,600 px of free band (≈ 5×). The band would pack
  within a few thousand MCS of the population appearing, limited only by the Medium-contact
  condition on the budget and by removal near the sheet. The 11-02 reason for keeping the base
  change in Phase 5 ("ECM smoke tests are easier to read with more producers") is reversed by this
  arithmetic; the other reason (unknown effect size of myofibroblast `G` 0.1 → 0.001: no run since
  the 09-17 rescale) stands.
